    📋 Sample Document Format

    Header: Your Form Title (at the very top, large font)

    Input fields: Full Name: (ends with colon)

    Input fields: Email Address: (ends with colon)

    Sub-heading: Personal Information (medium font)

    Input: Date of Birth: (ends with colon)

    Signature: Signature (contains the word "signature")

    Footer: Page 1 (at the bottom)

    ⚠️ Troubleshooting

    If scanning fails:

    • Ensure your PDF has selectable text (not scanned images)
    • Check that your DOCX file is not corrupted or password-protected
    • Try simplifying your document format if the scan fails
    • For PDFs, ensure they were created from a text editor, not scanned

    🔄 After scanning: The detected form blocks will appear in the builder. You can edit, reorder, or delete them as needed.

     About FormForge
    
    
    
      
      
      
      
    
    

    
    
      
    FormForge is a form builder and document scanner that lives entirely in your browser — build, preview, and export official forms as PDF, TXT, or DOCX without uploading anything.
    
      
    
        
     Quick start
    
        
      
          
    1. Add blocks — inputs, headings, tables, and numbered outlines from the Form builder panel.
      2. 
          
    2. Build & fill — switch between Build and Fill-up mode to preview the form as a user would see it.
      3. 
          
    3. Export — generate a PDF, TXT, or DOCX with one click. You can also Scan Doc a PDF/DOCX to auto-detect blocks.
      4. 
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
     Privacy
    
        
    Everything stays on your machine — blocks, fill-up values, and templates are saved only in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

     A little about me
    
        
    Hi — I’m the person behind FormForge, and no, I did not go to design school. I went to MIST (Military Institute of Science and Technology, Bangladesh), where they taught me a lot about engineering and approximately nothing about kerning or color theory. Everything you see here is self-taught, mildly obsessive, hobbyist-grade web design.
    
        
    What I do know is the special kind of soul-crushing tedium that comes from filling out the same official form by hand for the fifth time because you smudged a box or wrote the date wrong. One day I got fed up enough to build a tool instead of complaining about it. FormForge spits out clean, printable forms in seconds, and can even scan an existing paper form back into something editable — like a very patient intern who never messes up the alignment.
    
        
    It started as a weekend fix for my own paperwork problems and quietly turned into... this. I hope it saves you as much time (and printer ink) as it’s saved me.
